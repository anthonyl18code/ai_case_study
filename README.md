# AIRS Medical- Founded in 2018 by Hyeseong Lee, M.D & Jingu Lee
* AIRS Medicals vision is to transform healthcare through the power of AI and robotics. Their goal is prevent illness, not just treat it. 

* AIRS Medical, through their SwiftMR technology uses AI to shorten the time to scan patients during an MRI. This technology will not only increase patient comfort, allow more patients to be seen by clinicans, but also increase revenue for clinics across the globe. All of this while enhancing the images these MRIs produce, thus allowing physicians to catch ailments sooner increasing the chances of patient recovery. 

## Business Activites of AIRS Medical

* This company is funded through a Series C in which they raised $20 million dollars.

* The intended customer for this project is any radiology facility that provides MRIs to patients. Originally only getting FDA approval for brain scans (2021), AIRS Medical has recently gotten FDA approval for spine, knee, hip, shoulder, and ankle scans (2023). 

* AIRS Medical uses its SwiftMR technology to seperate itself from competitors. This technology requires less images (which shortens patient time in the scanner) and through the use of AI it enhances these images to provide better quality scans. 

* Not only can no other company do this, but SwiftMR software is compatiable with all the major competitors in the space. I.e. Philips, GE, Seimens, and Canon Medical.

## Revenue

* >In a white paper, ***Demostrating The Economic Value of SwiftMR***

* >In a one year period it showed a $271k increase in revenue with a ROI of 453%

* >In a one year period, with 12 scans per day, SwiftMR allowed for $204k additional revenue and ROI of 340%

* >In a one year period, with 18 scans per day, SwiftMR allowed for $340k additional revenue and an ROI of 566%
